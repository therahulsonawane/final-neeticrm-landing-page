The problem

Call counts do not tell you how good the calls are

Two callers can make the same number of calls and get very different results. Managers rarely have time to listen.

1

Calls are a black box

Nobody can listen to every call, so coaching is based on hunches.

2

Objections go unanswered

New callers struggle when buyers raise price, location or timing.

3

Training is generic

Without data, coaching cannot target the specific skill each caller lacks.

Score

A scorecard for every call

Recorded calls are analysed and scored on conversation quality: discovery, objection handling and next-step clarity, with a clear coaching opportunity flagged.

  • Conversation quality score per call
  • Discovery, objection handling and next-step clarity
  • Recorded calls with their quality review on the lead profile

Coach

An objection coach in every pocket

In the mobile app the objection coach explains what a buyer really means and what the agent can say, so callers are supported in the moment.

  • Explains the buyer’s objection in plain language
  • Shows what the agent can say next
  • Available inside the mobile app

Team

See who needs coaching, and on what

Team scorecards show calls, talk time, visits and bookings with a clear status: on track, improving or needs coaching. Target-versus-actual intelligence connects call quality to site visits and bookings.

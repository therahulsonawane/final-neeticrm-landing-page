Consistent funnel

The same stages for every project

Every project follows the same path from new lead to site visit to booking, so you can compare projects fairly and spot exactly where leads stall.

  • Leads from ads, portals, WhatsApp, forms and CSV import in one list
  • Buyer profiles and repeat-buyer recognition across projects
  • Lead assignment that respects who is on leave

Reporting

Reports you can share with developers

Funnel reports by source, project and salesperson show cost per lead, cost per site visit and cost per booking, without rebuilding spreadsheets each week.

  • Cost per lead, site visit and booking
  • Split by project, source and salesperson
  • Target versus actual for every team

Speed

Faster response, while your team is on calls

First-call timers keep response times short. WhatsApp flows follow up automatically while your team is on calls, and branch based on whether the buyer replied.

  • First-call timers you configure
  • WhatsApp flows that wait and branch
  • Tasks created for the team when a buyer replies

Team

A scorecard for every salesperson

Calls, talk time, visits and bookings against target, with on-track, improving and needs-coaching statuses. On the Sales Intelligence plan, AI call analysis helps managers coach the whole team.

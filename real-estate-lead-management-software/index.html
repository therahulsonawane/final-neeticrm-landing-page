The problem

Where leads actually get lost

Most leakage is not about lead volume. It is about what happens in the first hours and days.

1

Late first call

The lead waits while the team is busy or the enquiry sits in an inbox.

2

No owner

Nobody is clearly responsible, especially when the assigned agent is on leave.

3

No source data

You cannot tell which portal or campaign produces visits and bookings.

Capture

Every source in one list

Leads from every portal, ad platform and form arrive in one list. Each lead keeps its campaign and source, so reports can compare channels honestly.

  • Meta, Google Ads, 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, WhatsApp, website forms and CSV import
  • Buyer score, repeat-lead and SLA-breach flags
  • Buyer profile: BHK, budget and timeline

Respond

Close the response gap

The command centre puts the leads that need attention first, with first-call timers you configure, and assignment that knows who is in.

  • First-call timers on every new lead
  • Leave-aware assignment
  • Follow-up tasks and site visit scheduling with reminders

Measure

Know which sources pay off

Marketing funnel reports show cost per lead, cost per site visit and cost per booking by source and project. That is how you decide whether to spend more on a portal or fix what happens after the lead arrives.

  • Cost per lead, visit and booking
  • Split by source and project
  • Target versus actual

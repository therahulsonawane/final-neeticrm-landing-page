Questions, answered.

How is Neeti CRM different from a traditional CRM?

Alongside lead tracking and follow-up workflows, Neeti CRM brings AI-supported sales conversations and performance intelligence into the same sales process.

Can we start with just one user?

Yes. Plans can start with one user, with additional users added as your team grows.

When does the free trial begin?

The 15-day trial begins after essential CRM activation: agreed pipeline setup, integrations, workflows and initial team onboarding. Third-party approvals and timely access from your team may affect activation.

Will we see 30% more bookings in 15 days?

No specific uplift is guaranteed in a 15-day period. The 30% figure is our initial improvement target for testing over an appropriate measurement period. The trial helps you evaluate live usage and leading indicators, while booking cycles may take longer.

What are the subscription commitments?

Three months with 5% off, six months with 10% off, or twelve months with 15% off the standard per-user monthly rate, paid in advance.

Does Neeti CRM include AI call analysis?

AI call analysis is included in the Sales Intelligence tier, subject to agreed allowances and supported setup. Some premium AI capabilities may depend on launch readiness.

Can I connect my existing lead sources?

Neeti CRM supports Meta (Facebook & Instagram) lead ads, Google Ads, 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, WhatsApp, website forms including WordPress and Elementor, and CSV import. Specific sources and technical requirements are confirmed during the setup discussion.

Are the launch bonuses free?

The three sales-improvement bonuses are complimentary for eligible founding-customer Sales Intelligence packages during a genuine defined launch period or cohort.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Agents get the CRM on their phone with offline mode, push alerts for new leads and tasks, and one-tap call outcomes. Automatic call recording works on Android.

Is my data secure?

Each organisation’s data is kept separate, what a person can see depends on their role, and two-factor sign-in is available to every user.

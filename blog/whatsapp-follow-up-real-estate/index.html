Property Buyers Not Replying on WhatsApp? 8 Follow-Up Templates That Work

Buyers answer WhatsApp faster than calls, but random messages get ignored. Eight templates, when to send them and what to automate.

By Neeti CRM Team27 Nov 20264 min read

The problem: you send the brochure, the buyer reads it, and then nothing. Or one salesperson sends long paragraphs, another sends only a PDF, and a third forgets to follow up. The result is inconsistent and impossible to measure.

The fix is structure: short messages, each with one purpose, sent at the right moment. Replace anything in [brackets] with your details.

Three ground rules

  • Get consent and stop when asked.
  • Use approved templates for business-initiated messages where WhatsApp requires them.
  • Keep it short. One idea per message, and always end with a question or next step.

The 8 templates

1. Instant acknowledgement (automatic, within a minute) > Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in [Project], [Location]. I am [Salesperson] from [Company]. I will call you shortly. Meanwhile, here is the brochure: [link]

2. After a missed call > Hi [Name], I just tried calling about [Project]. When is a good time to speak today or tomorrow? Reply here with a time.

3. After a good call > Thanks for your time, [Name]. You are looking for a [2/3 BHK] around [budget] in [area]. I shortlisted [unit type]. Floor plan and price sheet: [link]

4. Site visit invitation > Hi [Name], would you like to see [Project] in person? I have [Day] at [time] or [Day] at [time]. Which suits you?

5. Visit reminder (day before and morning of) > Reminder: your visit to [Project] is at [time]. Location pin: [link]. Contact on site: [Name, number]. Reply if you need to reschedule.

6. After the visit > Thank you for visiting [Project] today, [Name]. Any questions I can help with? I can share a payment plan overview whenever you are ready.

7. Value nudge for silent leads > Hi [Name], a quick update on [Project]: [construction progress or new visit slots]. Happy to share details if useful.

8. Polite close > Hi [Name], I have not heard back, so I will assume the timing is not right. If plans change, message me here and I will be glad to help.

Automate the routine, personalise the rest

Messages 1, 2 and 5 follow fixed rules, so nobody should type them. In a CRM with WhatsApp automation you can build a flow: lead created, assign agent, send template, wait for reply, then notify the salesperson or create a call task.

Keep 3, 4 and 6 personal, since they follow real conversations. Our WhatsApp CRM page shows replies, brochures and visit buttons in one inbox.

Mistakes that kill replies

  • Walls of text
  • A brochure with no question
  • Messages at odd hours
  • Wrong name or project from copy-paste
  • Never stopping after a no

Why these templates work

Each one does a single job. The acknowledgement says you received the enquiry. The missed-call message lowers the effort of replying. The visit invitation offers two clear choices. The reminder removes uncertainty about where and when. The nudge gives a reason to respond. And the polite close often gets replies, because it takes the pressure off.

Make them sound human

Add the buyer's name and one detail from your conversation wherever you can, such as the unit type they asked about. Vary the wording between messages so a buyer who gets several does not feel they are reading a form letter. If a buyer replies, answer promptly in plain language and move the conversation toward a call or visit.

Finally, respect your buyer's time. Send messages in sensible hours, avoid back-to-back follow-ups and stop when asked.

Review replies, not just sends

Once a week, read a sample of buyer replies. They show what questions keep coming up, which you can answer in the next template.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send WhatsApp messages automatically?

Yes, through the WhatsApp Business platform. Business-initiated messages usually need approved templates and consent, so check current WhatsApp rules.

How many follow-ups are too many?

Space them out and give each a reason. Stop when the buyer asks you to or says they are not interested.

Should WhatsApp replace calling?

No. WhatsApp keeps buyers warm; the call is where you understand needs and handle objections.
